LocateGo clickable prototype

Tap through the app as a locate technician or a utility reviewer. Nothing is saved to a real system, and every ticket is made up.

Try these as a technician
  1. Start your shift (you'll see the tracking policy first) and look at My route, the stops dispatch planned for today.
  2. Go to stop 1, swipe to start the trip, simulate the drive, swipe to arrive. Turn on Utility records.
  3. Complete it: photos and results, the locate sketch, then sign and send the locate document.
  4. Watch for the emergency offer, try Report a delay from the ⋮ menu, and flip Simulate no signal in My work.
Try these as a utility reviewer
  1. Filter by ticket type. Send records for the Design & Planning ticket, or clear one without a visit.
  2. Answer the delay notice, check Locates expiring soon, and open the Dedicated Locator project.
  3. Rate a finished locate and open its locate document.

Prototype only. Sample data for testing. Drag to pan the maps, scroll or pinch to zoom.
